# 5. Major Projects

## A. CivicIQ

**Type:** AI-powered civic intelligence platform

### Problem

Traditional civic complaint systems mainly collect complaints but don't intelligently help authorities decide:

* Which complaint is most important?
* Which complaints are duplicates?
* Which department should handle it?
* Which issue affects the most people?
* What needs immediate attention?

### CivicIQ solution

AI-powered complaint triage system that can:

* Analyze complaints
* Calculate priority
* Detect duplicate/similar complaints
* Route complaints to the appropriate department
* Help authorities prioritize work
* Provide an intelligent dashboard
* Display civic information spatially

### Priority model discussed

Priority score was designed around factors such as:

* Severity
* Population affected
* Delay impact
* Weather/risk

Example weighting:

**Priority = Severity × 0.40

* Population Affected × 0.25
* Delay Impact × 0.20
* Weather/Risk × 0.15**

### Stack

* React
* Vite
* TypeScript
* FastAPI / backend services
* Groq
* Firebase / Firestore
* Google Maps
* Render
* Vercel / Netlify

CivicIQ became one of the major hackathon/product projects in the journey.
